# Your First Phrase

1. Open xpando and pick a folder on the left.
2. Press **New phrase**.
3. Under **Trigger**, type the short text you will type to get the phrase, for example `;sig`.
4. Optional: give the phrase a **Name** so you can find it in the list.
5. Under **Text**, type what you want xpando to put in. Several lines are fine.
6. Press **Save** (Ctrl+S).

![Editing a phrase](images/edit-phrase.png)

Now click in any program and type `;sig`. The trigger is replaced by your text.

## Triggers

* Use **Add another trigger** to give the same phrase more than one trigger.
* Start triggers with a symbol such as `;` or `:` so they do not appear in normal words by accident.
* xpando refuses to save while something is wrong and says why next to the buttons ("Can't save yet: ...").

## When it expands

Tick these boxes under **When it expands**:

* **Only expand when I type it as a whole word**: the trigger only works with a space or punctuation on both sides, so it does not fire inside another word.
* **Match the capitalization I type**: typing the trigger capitalised or in capitals gives a capitalised or all-capitals result.

## More options

Open **More options** for:

* **Description**: notes for yourself.
* **Search terms**: extra words that find this phrase when you search. They do not change what is typed.

The chips under **Options** on the details pane show what a phrase uses at a glance.

## Changing and deleting

* **Edit** opens the same form again.
* **Delete** asks you to confirm.
* Things the form cannot edit yet (for example regular expression triggers) are shown read-only and say so. Use [[Advanced Mode]] and [[Editing YAML]] for those.
